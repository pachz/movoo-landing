The **Shipment** page allows Merchants to view and track all shipments created from their connected sources.  
It provides filters to quickly view shipments by status:  
`All`, `Created`, `Accepted`, `Dispatched`, `Picked Up`, `In Transit`, `Delivered`, `Cancelled`, `Failed`.

Each row in the list includes:  
`Integration ID`, `Source`, `Destination`, `Shipment ID`, `Created Date`, `Status`, `COD`, `Last Updated`, `Action`.

From here merchants can monitor shipment lifecycles, review COD (Cash on Delivery) values,  
and open shipment details for deeper investigation when needed.

---

## Shipment Details

By clicking on the **Action** icon, merchants can open the **Shipment Details** page.  
This page provides a full view of the shipment, including:

- **Order Summary** — order ID, tracking ID, source, destination, notes, payment status, creation date.  
- **Order Status** — timeline showing when the order was created and subsequent status updates.  
- **Pickup Address** — store contact details, phone number, address, coordinates, and map view.  
- **Delivery Address** — customer contact details, phone number, address, coordinates, and map view.
