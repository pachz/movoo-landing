[Quick Delivery](https://www.quickdeliveryco.com/) is a fast and reliable delivery platform.  
By integrating **[Quick Delivery](https://www.quickdeliveryco.com/)** with **Muvx**, you can quickly create delivery destinations and manage orders efficiently.

---

## Integration Flow

1. **Register & Create Source**  
   Before connecting Quick Delivery, make sure you have at least one **Source** created in Muvx.  
   → See [Source Setup Guide](/documentation/admin-panel-manual-source-start) for instructions.

2. **Create Destination**  
   - From the **Catalog** page, browse the list of available Delivery Providers.  
   - Select **Quick Delivery**.  
   - In the integration form:
     - **Destination Name** : Internal label for your Quick Delivery destination.  
     - **Client ID**: Unique identifier for your client.  
     - **Client Code**: Code associated with your client for internal tracking.  
     - **Service Type**: Select your preferred delivery mode (**Flexible** or **Premium**) based on your Quick Delivery contract and service needs.
     - **Sources**: this Destination should connect to
   - Submit the form to complete integration.

3. **Complete Integration**  
   Once submitted, your **Destination** will be created and linked to the selected Sources.  
   → For more details, see [Destination Details Documentation](/documentation/admin-panel-manual-destination-details).

## Important Notes

- **Scheduled Pickup Handling**  
  Quick Delivery supports `scheduled Start Time`.  
  When you provide a `scheduled pickup time` in Muvx, the order is sent immediately to Quick Delivery with the corresponding `scheduled Start Time`. The pickup will occur according to your selected service type (**Flexible** or **Premium**) and the terms of your contract.  

- **No Post-Creation Cancellation**  
  Quick Delivery does **not** allow canceling an order once it has been sent.  
  Because Muvx sends the order immediately (whether it is a scheduled pickup or an instant order) **you will not be able to cancel the order at any time**.

### Delivery Services

Quick Delivery offers two types of delivery services:

- **Premium Service**
  - Standard weight limit per order: Up to 8 kg
  - Pricing: Based on total distance (in kilometers) from pickup to drop-off.
  - Order Quantity: No limit on the number of orders.
  - Service Type: On-demand; once the order is received, it is picked up and delivered immediately to the customer.

- **Flexible Service**
  - Standard weight limit per order: Up to 8 kg
  - Pricing: Flat rate (excluding remote areas).
  - Order Quantity: A minimum of 3 orders is required per pickup. You may send 3 or more orders.
  - Booking Slots: Delivery is based on the selected time slot. Two daily booking slots are available:
    - **Slot 1:** Orders booked before 3:00 PM will be delivered the same day.
    - **Slot 2:** Orders booked after 3:30 PM will be delivered either the same day or the next day.

<Banner title="Quick Delivery Service Type Setup">
  According to your Quick Delivery contract, when creating a Destination, you must select the service type (Flexible or Premium) as described above.  
  If your contract grants access to both service types and you want to use both, you must create two separate Destinations, one for Flexible and one for Premium.  
  Depending on your use case, you can then call the appropriate Destination via API or other integration methods.
</Banner>
