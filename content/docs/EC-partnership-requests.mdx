The **Partnership Requests** page shows all connection requests sent to you by delivery providers.  
Once you accept a request, the delivery provider will be linked to your **Source**.  

You can filter requests by:  
`All`, `Approved`, `Rejected`, `Pending`

Each row in the list includes:  
`Company Name`, `Request Sent On`, `Status`, `Last Status Update`, `Action`

From here you can:  
- Review partnership requests from delivery companies  
- Contact the provider directly to negotiate and finalize a contract  
- Approve or reject requests based on your agreement with the provider  

<Banner title="Important">  
Muvx acts only as an **integration layer**.  
All **contracts and agreements** with delivery providers must be handled directly between you and them.  
</Banner>
