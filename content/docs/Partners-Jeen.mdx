[Jeen](https://halajeen.com/) is a delivery platform designed for **efficient and reliable order fulfillment**.  
Integrating **[Jeen](https://halajeen.com/)** with **Muvx** allows you to manage all your deliveries in one place while keeping order statuses consistent across multiple providers.

---

## Integration Flow

1. **Register & Create Source**  
   Before connecting Jeen, you must have at least one **Source** created in Muvx.  
   → See [Source Setup Guide](https://www.muvx.ai/documentation/source-setup) for detailed instructions.

2. **Create Destination**  
   - From the **Catalog** page, browse the list of available Delivery Providers.  
   - Select **Jeen**.  
   - In the integration form:
     - Enter a **Destination Name** (internal label for your Jeen destination).  
     - Paste the **Merchant ID** obtained from Jeen.  
     - Choose which **Sources** this Destination should connect to.  
   - Submit the form to complete integration.

3. **Complete Integration**  
   Once submitted, your **Destination** will be created and linked to the selected Sources.  
   → For more details about Destinations, see [Destination Details Documentation](https://www.muvx.ai/documentation/destinations).

## Important Notes

- **No Native Scheduled Pickup**  
  Jeen does **not** support scheduling pickups for a future time (`scheduled_pickup_time`).  
  However, in Muvx you can still provide this field when creating an order.  
  - Muvx will **hold your order** until the scheduled time.  
  - At that time, Muvx sends the order to Jeen, and a driver will be assigned.

- **No Post-Creation Cancellation**  
  Jeen does **not** allow canceling an order once it has been created.  
  - If you use `scheduled_pickup_time`, you may still **cancel** the order *before* the scheduled pickup time is reached.  
  - After that, cancellation is no longer possible.  
  - For manual cancellation after creation, contact **Jeen Support**.
