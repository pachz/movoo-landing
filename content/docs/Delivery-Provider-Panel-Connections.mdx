## Business Partner (List)
The **Business Partner** page shows all partners your company is already connected to.

- Each row in the list includes:  
  `Company Name`, `Status`, `Number of Connections`, `Total Shipments`, `Action`

- From here you can:  
  - See which partners are currently active or inactive  
  - Track how many connections and shipments exist with each partner  
  - Click **Action → View Details** to open the **Business Partner Detail** page


## Business Partner Detail

The detail view is accessible from *Business Partner (List → Action: Open)*  
and contains four tabs:

#### A) Overview
The **Overview** tab gives a quick summary of this business partner’s performance and health.

- KPI cards show totals for:  
  `Created Orders`, `In Progress Orders`, `On Way Orders`, `Delivered Orders`

- A preview of **Integration Error Logs** lists:  
  `Company`, `Order ID`, `API Name`, `Status Code`, `Timestamp`

- From here you can:  
  - Monitor overall shipment flow at a glance  
  - Identify recent integration errors quickly  
  - Navigate to the full **Logs** page for detailed debugging  

#### B) Logs

The **Logs** section shows **all API interactions** related to this business partner.

Each log entry displays:

`Company`, `Order ID`, `API Name`, `Status Code`, `Timestamp`, `Path`, `IP Address`, `Details`


#### C) Shipment
The **Shipment** tab shows all shipments associated with this business partner.

- Filter shipments by status:  
  `Created`, `Accepted`, `Dispatched`, `Picked Up`, `In Transit`, `Delivered`, `Cancelled`, `Failed`

- Each row includes:  
  `Integration ID`, `Source`, `Shipment ID`, `Created Date`, `Status`, `COD (Cash on Delivery)`, `Last Updated`, `Details`

#### D) Settings

The **Settings** tab allows you to manage authentication and configuration for this business partner.  

Depending on the delivery provider’s system, the required credentials can vary. For example, one provider may only need an `API Key`, while another may require `Username` and `Password`

## Catalog

The **Catalog** page works as a marketplace where delivery providers can discover business partners **who have chosen to be visible**.

- From here you can:  
  - Browse available partners to expand your network  
  - Click **Send Request** to start the connection process  
  - After sending, the request appears in the **My Requests** page

### Connect Business (Send Request flow)


When you are in the **Catalog** page and click on the *Send Request* button for a business partner, you will be redirected here.  
In this screen you must provide the credentials that allow Muvx to integrate your system with the partner’s system.
This step is called **Connect Business** and it is required to activate the relationship.

<Banner title="Notes">  
  • Muvx is only an integration layer — all contracts and agreements with the partner must be handled directly between you and them.  
  • Submitting a request in the Catalog does not replace the need for a business agreement.  
</Banner>  

The required fields depend on the **structure of the delivery provider’s system** integrated with Muvx.  
They may include an `API Key`, `Username & Password`, or `Client ID & Secret`.  
Muvx automatically displays the correct fields for each provider in the form.  

After you submit the request:  
- The partner reviews your request and either approves or rejects it.  
- While the request is pending, you can still see its status in the **My Requests** page.  
- Once approved, the partner is added to your **Business Partner List** and you will be able to manage shipments, logs, and settings for that connection.  
- If rejected, the request remains visible in *My Requests* with a rejected status, and you can try again later with updated information.

## My Requests

The **My Requests** page shows all connection requests that have been sent to business partners and their current status.

- Filter requests by:  
  `All`, `Approved`, `Rejected`, `Pending`

- Each row in the list includes:  
  `Company Name`, `Request Sent On`, `Status`, `Last Status Update`, `Action`

- From here you can:  
  - Open a request when its status is **Approved** and view the **Business Partner Detail**  
  - Cancel a request if its status is **Pending**
