**[ososs](https://ososs.com/)** is an e-commerce platform that enables businesses to create and manage their online stores with powerful tools and seamless integrations.  
By connecting **[ososs](https://ososs.com/)** with **Muvx**, merchants can automate delivery operations and manage logistics with multiple delivery partners from a single dashboard.

---

## Integration Flow

1. **Sign Up on Ososs**  
   If you don't have an **Ososs** account yet, start by signing up on the [Ososs website](https://ososs.com) and complete your store setup.

2. **Sign Up on Muvx**  
   Go to [Muvx](https://www.muvx.ai) and create your account.  
   Once logged in, you'll have access to your **Muvx Dashboard**.

3. **Create a Source in Muvx**  
   Inside your Muvx panel:
   - Navigate to **Sources**.
   - Click on “Add Source”, then in the source catalog page, go to the Plugins section, select “ososs”, and create it.
   - After creating the source, copy the **API Key** generated by Muvx — you'll need it in the next step.

4. **Enable Muvx in Ososs Marketplace**  
   In your **Ososs dashboard**:
   - Open the **Marketplace**.
   - Search for **Muvx** and click **Install**.
   - During setup, you'll be asked to enter your **Muvx API Key**.
   - Paste the key you copied from Muvx, then complete the installation.

5. **Configure Destinations in Muvx**  
   Once the connection is active, you can manage your delivery setup directly in Muvx:
   - Go to **Destinations** in your Muvx panel.
   - Enable the delivery partners you've negotiated with.
   - Before going live, **switch the destination environment to "Production"**.  
     > By default, destinations are set to **Sandbox** mode for testing.

---

## Test Your Connection

Before going live, it's a good idea to make sure your Ososs–Muvx connection is working correctly.

1. In **Ososs**, create a **test order**.  
2. Go to your **Muvx Dashboard → Shipments**.  
3. You should see the test order appear automatically.  

If the shipment appears, your connection is successful!  
You can now switch your environment to **Production** and start managing real deliveries.

---

✅ **That's it!**  
Your **Ososs** store is now successfully connected with **Muvx**, and your delivery operations can run smoothly through your selected logistics partners.
