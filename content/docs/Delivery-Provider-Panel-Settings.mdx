## Billing

The Billing section manages your wallet balance, payments, and all charge activity on Muvx.  
Charges apply **only to delivery providers**. **Business Partners (ECs)** are not billed by Muvx.

Muvx uses a **prepaid wallet** model: you top up your wallet, and fees are **automatically deducted** as shipments are created/processed by your **Business Partners (ECs)** or when chargeable APIs are called.


---

### Overview

A snapshot of your billing status and the most recent ledger activity.

- **KPI Cards**:
  - **Wallet Balance** — Current available balance.
  - **Total Spent (30d)** — Charges deducted in the last 30 days.
  - **Last Recharge** — Amount and date of the latest top-up.

- **Recent Transactions**: a rolling table of your latest entries with:
  `Payment Date`, `Type`, `Amount`, `Status` (Success / Pending / Failed).

### Wallet

Add funds to your prepaid wallet so operations can continue without interruption.

- **Wallet Balance** — Displays current wallet balance.
- **Charge Wallet** — Choose a preset amount (e.g., `10 KWD`, `15 KWD`, `20 KWD`) or enter a **Custom** value, then confirm to top up.

<Image src="https://sawaai.lon1.digitaloceanspaces.com/bot/1757357292176_image_2025-09-08_22-18-10.png" alt="wallet" width="2876" height="1522" />


<Banner title="Tips">
  • Keep a safety buffer in your wallet to prevent failed deductions during peak hours.  
  • Large top-ups reduce the frequency of recharges and reconciliation overhead.  
</Banner>



---

### Transactions History

A complete ledger of all credits and debits on your account with powerful filters and export.

- **Search & Filters** — Search by `Transaction ID` or `Note`, filter by date range, then **Export Transactions** to CSV.
- **Table Columns**:
  `Payment Date`, `Transaction ID`, `Type`, `Amount`, `Status`, `Note`.

**Types** commonly include:
- **Top Up** — Funds added to wallet (credit).  
- **Payment** — Charges settled to Muvx (debit).  
- **Deduction** — Automatic fee deducted for a shipment/API (debit).  
- **Refund** — Funds returned to wallet (credit).

### Usage History

An audit trail of **chargeable API activity** and per-shipment deductions, grouped by day, with drill-down.

- **Filters** — Pick a date, search by `Shipment ID`.
- **Daily Summary Columns**:
  `Date`, `Total Shipments`, `Total Deduction`, `Balance After`, `Details` (Expand/Collapse).
- **Expanded Rows (per event)**:
  `Time`, `Shipment ID`, `API Called`, `Deducted`, `Balance After`.

Use **Export CSV** for reporting and reconciliation.

## Company Settings

The Company Settings section allows delivery providers to manage their organization profile and control who has access to the panel.  
It ensures that company details are up to date and that team members have the right roles for collaboration.

---

### Company Information

This page shows the registered details of your company in Muvx.  
Some fields are read-only (set during onboarding), while others can be updated by admins.

- **Company Logo** — Upload or change the official company logo (PNG, JPG, WebP up to 5MB, square format recommended).  
- **Company Name** — Displayed as the official company name (read-only).  
- **Company Slug** — A unique system identifier for your company (read-only).  
- **Website** — Public website of the company.  
- **Phone Number** — Contact number for operations.  
- **Address** — Physical office address.  
- **Location** — Country of operation, shown with flag.

Click **Edit** to update editable fields, then save changes.

### Team Members

Manage the list of people who have access to your company’s Delivery Provider Panel in Team Members tab.  

- **List View**: Each row shows the member’s name, email, and assigned role.  
- **Roles**:  
  - **Owner** — Has full control over the company account (cannot be removed).  
  - **Admin** — Can invite members, edit company information, and manage integrations.  
  - **Viewer** — Read-only access; can see data but cannot make changes.  

- **Invite Member**: Add a new team member by entering their email and selecting a role.  
    The user receives an **invitation email** and can also accept the invitation directly from their **User Settings** page. Once accepted, they appear in the list with the assigned role.
<Banner title="Notes">  
  • Only **Admins** and the **Owner** can manage team members.  
  • Assign roles carefully to maintain account security.  
  • Multiple admins can exist, but there is always exactly one owner.  
</Banner>
