Beep Beep is a delivery service API used for **urban logistics**.  
If you want to send your orders through **Beep Beep** using Muvx, please note:

- You must first **sign a contract directly with Beep Beep**.  
- All terms (such as delivery costs and conditions) are handled in that contract.  
- You must obtain your **API Key** from Beep Beep before integration.

---

## Integration Flow

1. **Register & Create Source**  
   Before connecting Beep Beep, you must have at least one **Source** set up.  
   → See [Source Setup Guide](admin-panel-manual-source-start).

2. **Create Destination**  
   - From the **Catalog** page, browse the list of available Delivery Providers.  
   - Select **Beep Beep**.  
   - In the integration form:
     - **Destination Name**: (internal label for your Beep Beep destination)  
     - **API Key**: you obtained from Beep Beep  
     - **Sources**: this Destination should connect to
   - Submit the form to complete integration.

   Once submitted, your **Destination** will be created and linked to the selected Sources.  
   → For more details about Destinations, see [Destination Details Documentation](admin-panel-manual-destination-details).

## Important Notes

- **No Native Scheduled Pickup**  
  Beep Beep does not support scheduling pickups for a future time (`scheduled_pickup_time`).  
  However, in Muvx you can still provide this field when creating an order.  
  - Muvx will **hold your order** until the scheduled time.  
  - At that time, Muvx sends the order to Beep Beep, and a driver will be assigned.

- **No Post-Creation Cancellation**  
  Beep Beep does not allow canceling an order once it has been created.  
  - If you use `scheduled_pickup_time`, you may still **cancel** the order *before* the scheduled pickup time is reached.  
  - After that, cancellation is no longer possible.
